The Problem

Open dashboard. Check numbers.
Close tab. Repeat.

As an indie founder you check your revenue dashboard up to 40× a day. It kills your flow. MRRbar fixes that — the number is always visible, right in your toolbar.

How it works
01 — INSTALL

Install the extension

Grab MRRbar from the Chrome Web Store. Takes under 60 seconds.

02 — CONNECT

Add your API key

Paste your Stripe read-only key and/or Lemon Squeezy key. Keys stay local on your device — always.

03 — DONE

Revenue in your toolbar

Your MRR or today's revenue appears instantly in the browser badge. Auto-refreshes every 5 min.

See it in action

Everything you need. At a glance.

Security & Privacy

Your keys. Your device.

🔐

Stored locally

API keys are only stored in chrome.storage.sync — encrypted by Chrome, never on external servers.

👁️

Read-only

Stripe restricted key with charges:read + subscriptions:read only. Lemon Squeezy read-only scope.

🚫

No CDN

No external scripts, no tracking, no CDN. Fully self-contained extension.

🇪🇺

GDPR-friendly

No data leaves your browser. The extension talks directly to Stripe / Lemon Squeezy APIs only.
